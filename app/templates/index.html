How to Train Recognition

  1. Create folders under data/known/<person_name>/.
  2. Place 3–10 clear JPGs of each person (frontal face preferred).
  3. Restart the server. The recognizer will train on startup.
If no training images found, the app will still detect faces and label them as face.
